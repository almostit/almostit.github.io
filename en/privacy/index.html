Privacy Policy

Game data, Apple purchases, advertising, support and your rights.

Version: 5 October 2026

1. Controller and scope

The controller for processing carried out by Almost It? is Wladyslas Leforestier, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel — EI), trading as Almost It?.

Privacy contact: almostit.app@gmail.com.

Business address: 10 rue Herold, 75001 Paris, France.
Telephone: +33 6 95 11 02 66.

This policy covers the iOS app and this website. Almost It? is a local game for 3–20 players, intended for people aged 16 and over. You do not need an Almost It? account to play. Apple and Google also process some data for their own services, under their own policies.

2. Game data and settings on your device

Player names or nicknames, the current game, scores, history and settings are stored locally on your device to run and resume the game. They are not sent to an Almost It? gameplay server: there is no gameplay backend. A nickname is enough; do not enter sensitive information as a player name.

This supports delivery of the game you request (performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Other participants’ details are entered by the person setting up the game; inform them and use their nicknames with their agreement. People using the phone can access this information.

The data stays on your device until you erase it in the app’s Settings or delete the device’s data. Any device backups depend on your Apple settings. Almost It? cannot remotely view or erase a game stored only on your phone. Erasing local data does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple purchases.

3. Apple purchases and Premium access

StoreKit provides the app with product, transaction and entitlement information needed to activate Premium, check a subscription or restore a purchase. The app stores the state needed for access locally; its validity depends on the purchase, subscription, refund or revocation status supplied by Apple. This processing is necessary to perform the contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

Apple receives and processes account, payment and transaction data through the App Store. Almost It? does not receive your card number. Apple retains its records according to its service needs and obligations, including accounting and fraud prevention. Deleting the app does not delete those records.

Read Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising in Free

Free uses Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the UMP choice platform. The publisher first-party ID is enabled. Premium does not display advertising.

Depending on the SDK configuration and your choices, Google and the relevant advertising partners may process IP addresses and network information, technical identifiers associated with the device, app or SDK, ad interactions, diagnostics and performance data. An IP address may be used to estimate a general location. These operations support ad delivery and measurement, SDK operation and abuse prevention. Almost It? does not use game data to build advertising profiles.

Where consent is required, UMP collects your choices before the relevant processing. You can revisit them in the app’s privacy options when available. Consent is then the basis for the relevant processing (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)); withdrawal applies going forward. The form identifies purposes, partners and, where applicable, processing based on legitimate interests with a right to object. Declining personalisation does not turn Free into ad-free Premium.

Choices are retained by the SDK mechanisms until they are changed, expire or are requested again. Advertising data retention varies by purpose and partner, as described in the choice interface and partner policies; Almost It? does not set one retention period for all Google processing.

Google Privacy Policy · Google Mobile Ads data disclosure documentation.

5. Optional local notifications

iOS reminders are scheduled on your device according to your choices and iOS permission. These reminders do not use a notification backend or a remote push token. You may disable them in the app or iOS settings without losing access to the game. Settings and scheduled reminders stay local until changed, cancelled or erased.

6. Support messages

If you email almostit.app@gmail.com, we process your email address, message, chosen attachments and technical information you voluntarily provide, to reply and resolve the issue. Do not send passwords or payment card details. A reply address and a description of the issue are needed to handle your request; other information is optional.

The basis is performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps for service-related requests (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), or our legitimate interest in answering other requests and defending our rights (Article 6(1)(f)). Messages are accessible to the publisher and the Google/Gmail email provider, and to authorised authorities or advisers if an obligation or dispute requires it.

Support correspondence is kept for up to 24 months after the last exchange. Necessary information may be retained longer to meet a legal obligation or defend rights, for the period required by that obligation or dispute.

7. This website and Firebase Hosting

This site includes no Analytics, ads, custom tracker or Firebase frontend SDK. It only remembers your FR/EN choice in your browser’s local storage, using “almost-it-site-language”, to display the requested language. This preference remains until replaced or the site’s data is cleared. Our script does not send it to a server. You can clear it in your browser; navigation remains possible without this storage.

The website uses Firebase Hosting (Google LLC). The host processes incoming IP addresses to serve the site, detect abuse and provide infrastructure usage information. Firebase documentation states that Hosting retains IP data for a few months. For Almost It?, hosting and security operations rely on the legitimate interest of providing a safe, accessible website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Technical connection information is necessary to access the site.

Firebase privacy and security information, including Hosting retention.

8. Recipients, transfers and limits

Game data remains local. For other functions, recipients are the publisher, Apple for purchases, Google for advertising, choice management, hosting and email, and the advertising partners identified in the privacy options, depending on applicable purposes and choices.

These providers use international infrastructure; processing may occur outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Applicable safeguards are described in their policies: depending on the recipient and transfer, adequacy decisions (including the EU–US framework for certified organisations) or standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about safeguards for processing under our responsibility.

The V1 app does not include Firebase Analytics, PostHog, Sentry or Crashlytics. This does not mean that Apple or Google SDKs process no data. Almost It? does not implement automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects; any ad selection is described by Google and the privacy choices.

9. Your rights

Depending on the processing and legal conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. You may withdraw consent going forward without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Local-only data can be managed on your device; data specific to your Apple or Google account can also be managed with those providers.

Email almostit.app@gmail.com or write to the controller’s postal address with your request. Proportionate identity verification may be requested where needed. We reply within the statutory time limits, normally one month; a justified extension may be needed for a complex request. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French) without giving up other remedies.
