Frequently asked questions

How many players do we need?

Gather 3 to 20 players around a single phone.

What’s the aim of the game?

Accomplices share a word. Intruders have a similar one. Give clues and vote to unmask the Intruders. When Silence appears, that player has to improvise without a word.

Can we play in French and English?

Yes. The app offers French and English. Choose your language in Settings.

Can someone join or leave a game?

Yes, from the active game’s player screen. Changes take effect from the next round. Points already earned stay in the standings.

Where can I download the app?

The app has not launched yet. The official link will be added here when it becomes available on the App Store.

What age is the game for?

Almost It? is intended for people aged 16 and over.

What does Free include, and do I need Internet?

Free includes 6 categories, 50 word pairs per category and language, totalling 300 per language. It includes ads and requires Internet access. Losing connectivity pauses further play without erasing your game.

What does Premium add?

Monthly and Lifetime provide the same benefits: 200 word pairs per category and language, totalling 1,200 per language, with no ads and offline play. Monthly is a subscription; Lifetime is a one-time purchase. There is no promise of new content every month.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Premium and tap “Restore purchases”, with Internet access and the Apple account used for the purchase. Do not buy again to try to restore access. If the issue persists, contact support without sharing a password.

Does Lifetime cancel Monthly?

No. Lifetime takes priority for in-app access but does not cancel Monthly. Manage renewal separately in your Apple subscriptions. Apple cancellation instructions.

How do I request a refund?

For an Apple-billed transaction, follow Apple’s refund process. Statutory guarantees and other rights remain protected. See the Terms or contact us about an app defect.

How can I erase my data?

Use the local data erasure option in the app’s Settings. This affects local game data; it does not cancel Monthly or delete Apple purchases.

Are notifications required?

No. Reminders are optional and scheduled locally on the iPhone. You can disable them in the app or iOS settings.

How can I revisit my advertising choices?

Open the privacy options in the app when available. Declining personalisation does not unlock Premium. Read about data and your choices.
