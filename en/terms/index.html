2. Free, Monthly and Lifetime

  • Free: 6 categories, 50 word pairs per category and language, totalling 300 per language; advertising and an Internet connection are required to play.
  • Premium Monthly: an auto-renewing subscription, with a reference price in France of €2.99 per month; 200 word pairs per category and language, totalling 1,200 per language, without ads and with offline play while the entitlement is active.
  • Premium Lifetime: a one-time, non-consumable purchase, with a reference price in France of €14.99; the same Premium features with no renewal. It permanently unlocks these features and the content supplied in the app.

There is no separate “Remove Ads” offer. Monthly does not promise new content every month. The selected language does not change your Apple billing country. The applicable price and payment terms are those Apple displays before confirmation and may vary by country and account.

Lifetime does not automatically cancel Monthly. If you hold both, the app gives priority to Lifetime access, but you must manage Monthly renewal separately with Apple.

3. Supply, compatibility and connectivity

Before downloading or purchasing, check the iPhone/iOS compatibility requirements on the App Store listing and the information shown in the app. Purchases and restoration require Internet access and the corresponding Apple account. Your network provider may charge for connectivity.

Premium features activate when Apple confirms the purchase entitlement. Lifetime is a one-off supply of the unlock and local content; Monthly provides continuous access throughout each active monthly period and its renewals. The statutory guarantee period is not an expiry date for Lifetime access.

In Free, losing connectivity pauses further play without erasing the game. Premium allows offline play. Games, scores and settings stay local; erasing local data does not delete or cancel Apple purchases. Reminder notifications are optional and local.

4. Payment, renewal and restoration

Apple displays the offer and amount before confirmation, processes payment and supplies the transaction receipt. Monthly renews automatically unless cancelled with Apple before renewal. Cancellation stops future renewals; access remains active until the end of the covered period, subject in particular to refunds or revocation.

To recover a purchase, use “Restore purchases” in the Premium panel with the Apple account used to buy it. Restoration is not a new purchase. If you need help, contact support without sending a password.

For digital content not supplied on a physical medium, withdrawal rights are lost only when the legal conditions are met, including supply starting with your prior express consent, your acknowledgement of the loss of that right and the required confirmation. For a service started at your express request before the withdrawal period ends, the statutory early-performance rules apply; a proportionate amount may be payable where the legal conditions are met. These mechanisms do not remove statutory guarantees for defects.

For an Apple-billed transaction, use Apple’s refund request process and the withdrawal arrangements in its Media Services Terms for France (French). You may also contact us about the app, a guarantee or help with the process. Apple’s policies or decisions do not restrict mandatory remedies against the responsible trader.
